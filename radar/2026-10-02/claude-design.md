Claude Design is an Anthropic Labs product, launched in research preview in April 2026, for creating visual work together with Claude: designs, interactive prototypes, slides and one-pagers. It turns prompts, images, documents and codebases into a first version that you refine through conversation, direct edits or adjustment controls, and it can apply your own design system.

#### Should be used in a new project if:

* You need quick prototypes, mockups or pitch material early in a project, for example during pre-sales or discovery.

#### Should not be used in a new project if:

* You need production design files that the design team will maintain. We are still trialling it, and Figma remains the reference.
