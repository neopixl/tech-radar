Gradle Convention Plugins allow teams to extract and share common build logic across modules into reusable plugins. Instead of copy-pasting the same Gradle configuration into every module, a convention plugin centralises rules (Android setup, linting, testing, etc.) in one place, making multi-module builds easier to maintain and consistent.
