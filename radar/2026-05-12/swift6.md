Swift 6 introduces strict concurrency checking enforced at compile time, eliminating entire classes of data-race conditions. It builds on the structured concurrency model (async/await, actors) introduced in Swift 5.5 and makes safe concurrent code the default rather than an opt-in. Adopting Swift 6 requires resolving concurrency warnings progressively, but it results in more robust, thread-safe codebases.
