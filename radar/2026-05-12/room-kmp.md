Room for Kotlin Multiplatform extends Google's battle-tested Room persistence library to work across Android and iOS targets. It brings the same annotation-based DAO pattern, type-safe queries, and migration support that Android developers know, now usable from shared KMP modules backed by SQLite on both platforms.
